Privacy

This site is a school project for a class trip to Porto.

What we store

  • Your email and password, used to sign in. Accounts are created by the site owner.
  • The photos you upload, with the upload date and your account ID.

Photos are compressed in your browser before upload, which also removes hidden data such as the location where they were taken.

Why

Only to run the private class photo album.

Who can see it

  • A new photo is visible only to you and the admin until it is approved.
  • An approved photo is visible to everyone with an account.
  • Photos are never shown to visitors who are not signed in.

Services we use

Accounts and photos are stored with Google Firebase. The site is hosted on Vercel. The map uses Leaflet (loaded from unpkg) and Esri satellite images. These services receive your IP address when your browser connects to them.

Cookies

We use no analytics and no advertising cookies. Your browser keeps you signed in using its local storage, until you sign out.

Photos of other people

Only upload a photo of someone if they agree to it.

How long we keep it

Accounts and photos are deleted when the class project ends.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, including any photo of you. Write to valeriikravchenko6@gmail.com.
